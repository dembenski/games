⚠️ This mini-game is DIFFERENT from the full game — it's a bite-size remix. In the full game you're a cockroach loose in a huge open city with 13 weapons, driveable beetle cars, enforcer bugs and the Martial Law endgame. Here you get the pure squash-and-run chaos in one small city. Squash the ant civilians, stack combo multipliers up to x5, and grab the cash — but every 5 takedowns adds a WANTED star, and the ladybug police ram harder with every star. Hide in a park to cool off, hijack a beetle car, grab power-up orbs, and whatever you do, do not get stomped by the Exterminator's Pet at 5 stars.
